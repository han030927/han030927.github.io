**Xu Han is a co-first author.**

Published online in **IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (TGRS)** on **5 November 2026**, as an Early Access article.

GFFNet uses LiDAR-derived geometry to adapt the aggregation of neighbouring hyperspectral features. It combines nonlinear embeddings with spatial and frequency-domain modelling for land-cover classification.
